Need to Know More?

Questions You May Have

Here are a few things homeowners often ask before starting a fabrication project.

What types of grill fabrication work do you do?
We fabricate main gates, window grills, balcony railings, staircase railings, compound gates and grills, and custom metalwork designed around your requirements.
Can I choose my own gate or grill design?
Yes. You can share your own design idea or reference images, and we will work with you to adapt it to your space and budget.
How much does a custom gate or grill cost?
Cost depends on size, design complexity, material, and finishing. Share your requirements through the form and we will provide an estimate.
Do you take measurements before starting the work?
Yes. We check measurements on site before fabrication begins, so the final work fits your space accurately.
How long does fabrication take?
Timelines vary with the scope of the project. We will confirm an estimated schedule once the design and measurements are finalised.
Do you provide installation services?
Please reach out through the form or WhatsApp to confirm installation support for your project.
How can I request a quote?
Fill in the Request a Free Estimate form above, or message us on WhatsApp. We will get back to you to discuss your project.
